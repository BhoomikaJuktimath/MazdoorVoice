# 60-second Demo Script

**0–8 sec:** “A lot of daily-wage workers finish the day without an easy record of days worked, agreed wages or partial payments. MazdoorVoice gives them a record they can understand.” Show the dashboard and clearly labelled sample account.

**8–20 sec:** Open Record Work, choose Kannada, record a short entry or upload audio. Explain that live transcription requires a configured Gnani API key; demo mode never fakes a transcript.

**20–30 sec:** With live credentials, show the Prisma transcript and edit a word if needed. Emphasise that the worker reviews it.

**30–40 sec:** Show editable workplace, days, daily wage and advance. Confirm and save. Point out the calculated expected wage and recorded balance.

**40–50 sec:** Add a confirmed payment in Payment Tracker. Show the updated work history and wage analysis.

**50–60 sec:** Ask the assistant “How much is still pending?” and show the record-based answer. With Timbre configured, play its spoken version. Close: “MazdoorVoice — Every Worker Deserves a Record.”
